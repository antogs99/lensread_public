# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026
**App:** LensRead
**Developer:** antogs99
**Contact:** antogs99@gmail.com

## 1. Summary

LensRead is a teleprompter app. There is no account, no sign-in and no server that stores your scripts, voice or videos. Your scripts and settings stay on your device. The only data we collect is anonymous usage analytics, described in section 5.

## 2. Information You Create

Scripts you type, paste or import are stored locally on your device. We do not receive them, and they are not uploaded or synced anywhere by LensRead.

## 3. Permissions and Device Features

- **Microphone and Speech Recognition (Voice-Follow).** Used to follow your place in the script while you read. Speech recognition is performed by Apple's speech framework, on your device where your device and language support it. Where on-device recognition is not available, iOS may send audio to Apple for processing under Apple's own privacy terms. LensRead does not record, store or receive your audio or transcripts.
- **Camera.** Used only when you record a video inside LensRead.
- **Photos (add only).** Used only to save videos you record to your Photo Library. LensRead cannot read your existing photos.
- **Files.** If you import a script, LensRead reads only the file you choose.

You can turn off any permission in iOS Settings. Without microphone or speech permission, the app falls back to timed scrolling.

## 4. In-App Purchases

LensRead Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple tells the app whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple ID so that it can restore your purchase.

## 5. Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It collects events such as screens viewed, features used (for example, starting the prompter or opening the paywall), purchase outcomes, app version, device model, operating system version, approximate region, and a random app-instance identifier generated for the app installation.

- This data is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. We do not collect your scripts, voice, recordings or contacts.
- It is used for analytics only and is not used for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.
- Google processes this data under its own privacy terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third parties involved are Apple (speech recognition, purchases, App Store) and Google (analytics, as described above).

## 7. Children's Privacy

LensRead is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Scripts and settings are stored on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. Analytics data is retained by Google Analytics according to the retention settings we choose, which are limited in time. Because analytics data is not linked to your identity, we cannot look up or delete data for a specific person. Deleting the app stops further collection from that installation.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information. Because LensRead does not collect personal information that identifies you, there is generally nothing for us to access or delete, but you can contact us at any time with questions or requests.

## 10. Security

Scripts are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are protected by iOS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it was last changed. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
