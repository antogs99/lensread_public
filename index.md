# LensRead Support

LensRead is a voice-following teleprompter for iPhone and iPad.

**Need help, found a bug, or have an idea?** Email **antogs99@gmail.com**. We read every message.

## Common questions

**How do I restore my purchase?** Open Settings in LensRead and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

**Voice-Follow isn't scrolling.** Check that LensRead has Microphone and Speech Recognition permission in iOS Settings. Without them the app falls back to timed scrolling.

**Can I import a Word file?** Not yet. Export it as PDF or TXT, or paste the text into a new script.

**Where are my scripts stored?** On your device only. Keep a copy of anything important.
